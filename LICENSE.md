# NativeUI usage policy

NativeUI is free to use for open source projects, whether commercial or
non-commercial.

NativeUI is also free to use for hobby projects, including closed-source
hobby projects.

For closed-source commercial use, contact
[nativeui@xtsoftwarelabs.com](mailto:nativeui@xtsoftwarelabs.com) to get a license.

This policy applies to the NativeUI compiler and runtime distributed in this
repository's releases. It does not change the licenses of your application or
its dependencies. Third-party components retain their own licenses.
